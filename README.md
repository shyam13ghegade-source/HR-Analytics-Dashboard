# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze employee attrition and workforce data. It helps HR teams understand employee turnover, demographic patterns, job roles, education levels, and salary distribution through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard presents key HR metrics in a clear and easy-to-understand format to support data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Understand attrition patterns across different age groups.
- Analyze employee education and gender distribution.
- Compare attrition across job roles and salary ranges.
- Present HR metrics through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Help HR teams identify areas for further investigation and improvement.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and analysis

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Employee Attrition Analysis
Shows employee attrition counts and the overall attrition rate.

### 2. Age Group Analysis
Visualizes attrition across different employee age groups to identify patterns in workforce turnover.

### 3. Education Analysis
Displays attrition by educational background, including Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, Technical Degree, and other categories.

### 4. Gender Analysis
Shows the distribution of attrition between male and female employees.

### 5. Job Role Analysis
Compares attrition across different job roles, including Laboratory Technician, Sales Executive, Research Scientist, and Sales Representative.

### 6. Salary Analysis
Shows attrition across salary ranges to support further investigation of compensation-related patterns.

## Key Observations

- The dashboard reports 238 employee attritions.
- The displayed attrition rate is 16.1%.
- The 26–35 age group has the highest attrition count among the age groups shown.
- Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives have relatively high attrition counts among the listed job roles.
- The dashboard enables HR teams to explore attrition patterns by age, education, gender, job role, and salary range.

These observations describe the displayed data and do not establish the reasons employees leave.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the reasons for attrition among younger employees.
- Review employee feedback and retention practices in job roles with higher attrition.
- Examine compensation, career development, and work satisfaction alongside attrition data.
- Monitor attrition trends regularly to evaluate HR initiatives.
- Use the dashboard to support evidence-based workforce planning.
